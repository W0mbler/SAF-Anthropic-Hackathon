SAF COMMAND · 2/6
02 · Method

A real mixed-integer model.

Biomass sources→Depots→Refineries→Airports

Objective

Facility CapEx + discounted recurring transport and configured processing costs.

Constraints

Supply, capacities, exclusive sizes, mass conservation, conversion yield and exact airport deliveries.

SciPy / HiGHS chooses facilities and quantities. Status, gap and assumptions remain visible.
